# Fraize Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 25/08/2026*

Fraize is designed to work entirely on your device. We don't run any servers, collect any personal data, or track your usage.

## What we store, and where

Everything Fraize saves - your streak, your progress, starred phrases, custom phrases you create, your name, your trip details, game high scores - stays locally on your device. None of it is sent to us or anyone else. We have no servers and no way to see this information.

## Microphone and speech recognition

If you use Fraize's speak-to-answer feature, your device's microphone and speech recognition are used to turn what you say into text, so your answer can be checked. Fraize does not record or store your voice. On Android, speech recognition may be processed by your device's speech recognition service (typically provided by Google as part of Android), which is outside Fraize's control - this happens at the operating system level, the same as it would for any app using Android's built-in speech recognition.

## Third-party services

Fraize does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
