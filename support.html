Frequently Asked Questions

Does Fraize work offline?
Yes. Fraize is designed to work fully offline once installed, so you can use it while travelling without needing data or wifi.

Do I need an account?
No. Fraize doesn't require sign-up, and everything is stored locally on your device.

I found a bug or something isn't working. What should I do?
Please email us at the address above with a short description of what happened, and which device and app version you're using if possible. We read every message.
